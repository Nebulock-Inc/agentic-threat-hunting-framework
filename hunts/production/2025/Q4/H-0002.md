# H-0002: AWS IAM Policy Abuse Baseline

## Objective

Establish baseline for IAM policy modifications and identify abnormal privilege escalation patterns across AWS accounts.

## Investigation Summary

**Outcome:** Control Gap Identified

### Baseline Established

- **Normal IAM operations:** 145 policy changes/week
- **Primary actors:** DevOps team (12 users)
- **Peak activity:** Tuesday-Thursday, 9am-5pm EST
- **Common operations:** AttachUserPolicy (45%), CreateRole (30%), PutRolePolicy (25%)

### Control Gap

**Finding:** No alerting on cross-account assume role policy modifications
- **Risk:** Attackers could grant themselves cross-account access
- **Recommendation:** Implement detection for AssumeRolePolicyDocument changes
- **Priority:** Medium

### Queries Executed

```sql
-- IAM policy modifications baseline
SELECT 
    event_name,
    user_identity,
    COUNT(*) as operations,
    DATE_TRUNC('hour', event_time) as hour
FROM cloudtrail_events
WHERE event_name IN ('AttachUserPolicy', 'PutUserPolicy', 'CreateRole')
AND event_time >= DATE_SUB(NOW(), INTERVAL 30 DAY)
GROUP BY 1,2,4
ORDER BY operations DESC;
```

## Business Impact

Identified control gap in cross-account IAM monitoring. Documented baseline for future anomaly detection. No active threats detected.
